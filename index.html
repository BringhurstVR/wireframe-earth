🌐 Wireframe Earth

Stand on a true-scale wireframe planet. Look down through the Earth to see where far-off places really sit below your horizon. Point a controller at the planet for an info bubble, push the thumbstick up/down to rise and sink, and watch nearby places sink as you descend.
